Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Guidance ("Guidance," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at guidances.net and our mobile apps (together, the "Service"). Guidance is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY — e.g. your name or company].

Guidance is not a medical service. It is a self-reflection and journaling tool, not therapy and not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or, in the US, call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). See our Terms of Service for more.

The short version

  • You can use Guidance's offline mode to generate affirmations entirely within your own browser or device — that text is not sent to us.
  • If you save an entry to your journal, the text you wrote and the affirmation are stored securely in the cloud, tied to your private account.
  • If you use AI mode, the text you wrote is sent to our server and to Anthropic (the makers of Claude) to generate a response for you.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use your journal entries to advertise to you.

Information we collect

Account information

To create an account, we use Firebase Authentication. Depending on how you sign in, we receive:

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, and profile photo as provided by Google.
  • Phone sign-in: your phone number, which we use to send a one-time verification code by SMS.

We also store a unique account identifier (a Firebase user ID) to associate your account with your journal.

Your journal entries

When you choose to save an entry, we store it in Google Cloud Firestore under your account. Each saved entry includes the text you wrote, the affirmation and reflection generated for you, a theme label, and a timestamp. These entries are private to your account. You can delete individual entries at any time from within the app.

Content processed by AI mode

If you use the optional AI mode, the text you write is transmitted to our backend and then to Anthropic, which operates the Claude model that composes your affirmation. Anthropic processes this text as our service provider in order to return a response. We do not use this content for advertising. In offline mode, no entry text is sent to us or to Anthropic — the affirmation is generated on your device.

Technical and usage information

Like most online services, our providers automatically collect limited technical data needed to operate the Service securely — for example, IP address, device and browser type, and server logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) and Firebase process this data on our behalf.

reCAPTCHA

Phone sign-in is protected by Google reCAPTCHA to prevent abuse. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information (such as device and application data) and sends it to Google for analysis. Your use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
